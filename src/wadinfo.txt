Description             : Interception III started development privately on
                          August 19, 2021. To mark 5 years of work on this
                          project, I've compiled a snapshot build of *most* of
                          the work that's gone into this wad.

                          I say most because some mappers are still working out
                          their contributions. Think of this as an open beta
                          or early release rather than a release candidate.

                          The wad stands at 36 levels, including secret and
                          bonus levels. This behemoth will likely take you
                          hours to complete on a first playthrough and gets
                          tough as hell.

                          That being said, I think the strengths of this
                          project far outshine the difficulty and length.
                          Here stands a wad that embraces magnum opus syndrome,
                          asking you, the player, to invest a fair bit of time.
